# MolReader Diagnosis

Locate defects and organize evidence. Report current risks, measurements and interpretation limits. Keep molecular edits and reward selection out of DiagnosticReport. Record cross-time precursor hypotheses in a separate evidence artifact. Chinese instructions: [SKILL.zh-CN.md](SKILL.zh-CN.md).

## Native residual needs first

When raw references are configured, begin with inspect_raw_comparison(section="residual_needs"): explicit final remainder, then current controllable precursor. Inspect all selected nodes using their own chemistry, relation applicability, measurements, references and deviations. Persistent, recurrent, late-emergent, naturally resolved, absent relation and missing observation remain distinct. Chemistry changes do not erase defects under the new type. Without an explicit raw final, terminal status is unknown.

Record candidate coordinate mechanisms, useful contact/conformation changes to preserve, correlations, competing explanations and missing local measurements in the separate temporal analysis. Host initializes the evidence index and current diagnosis; there is no mandatory tool-read order. Use existing calculators through measure_evidence_gap and read_measurement_supplement. Supplements have source, coordinate and configuration bindings and never replace original packet/data. Report missing capability or inputs explicitly. Future evidence cannot authorize a current numerical reference. Biology chooses and ranks interventions.

## Ground the report

Verify the packet identity, input provenance, coordinate frame and original zero-based atom slots. Keep current noisy state, predicted endpoint and decoded molecule explicit. Bind every claim to packet evidence. New measurements require a new evidence packet before they enter the report.

Use the [report contract](references/report-contract.md) for evidence organization and [risk interpretation](references/risk-interpretation.md) when chemical or geometric meaning is ambiguous.

## Build local risk cards

1. Lead with valence/charge conflicts, local geometry, severe collisions, disconnection and input integrity. State atom identities and location, bond/angle roles, measured values, units, references and evidence identifiers.
2. Merge prediction/SDF observations only when atom mapping, chemistry and coordinates match. Retain each source. Attach the same PoseBusters signal as supporting evidence without counting another defect.
3. Merge overlapping PAINS/BRENK matches into one regional screening concern. Preserve rule names and distinguish disconnected sites. Catalog names do not establish chemical impossibility or measured inactivity.
4. Explain category alternatives, stage changes and strain beside the affected risk. Preserve competing probabilities. Do not turn confidence into failure probability or global energy into local energy attribution.
5. Separate noisy-state observations. Highlight independently localizable contradictions, unresolved identities and severe overlaps; place numerous graph-dependent consequences in an evidence appendix.
6. Compare chemistry before claiming persistence across stages. The same slots can change elements, charges, bonds and angle-center roles. A repeated region can represent a different chemical constraint.

## Control information density

Consolidate repeated missing or partial measurements by root cause, keeping affected observations traceable. Unavailable docking, unprepared interaction typing and unvalidated receptor chemistry are coverage limits, not molecular defects.

Keep routine molecular weight, logP, QED, affinity predictions and telemetry in StatePacket unless a supplied target or a specific risk makes them relevant. Zero violations require a valid reference and a nonzero evaluated denominator.

Rank illustrative observations by severity and violation magnitude while retaining every material risk and source identifier in the structured report or appendix. State shared limitations once and local parameter caveats beside their risk. Do not assign calibrated probabilities without a calibration model.

## Deliver and verify

Generate English and Chinese Markdown from the same structured report. Preserve the same identities, measurements and evidence links in both languages. Verify evidence conservation, unique primary assignment, coverage grouping and separation of representations. Return report paths and the main present risks, without repair suggestions.
