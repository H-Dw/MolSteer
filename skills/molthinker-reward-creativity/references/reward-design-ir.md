## Computational contract

Deliver one scalar expression and its component/rank composition, chemical interpretation rules, coordinate mapping, explicit fixed variables and one external guidance_weight. Executor runs every remaining native step with the same reward, pulls through the same forward, preserves native sampling, and adds weight times native dt times the gradient. Natural zero gradients are valid; missing required components or nonfinite calculations fail with saved recoverable state. Legacy controller programs are readable historical artifacts and require scalar redesign before execution.

Keep source consistency, coordinate-copy derivatives, optional live pullback and terminal experiments as distinct validation scopes.
