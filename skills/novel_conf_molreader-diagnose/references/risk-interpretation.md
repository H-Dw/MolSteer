# Interpreting molecular risk evidence

Valence contradictions at known atoms can remain informative even when another atom is unresolved. Distinguish declared bond-order sum from chemically admissible valence and state the conservative reference used.

A short 1–3 endpoint distance couples bond lengths and bond angle; it does not isolate angular strain. Local force-field equilibrium geometry is a parameter-dependent reference, not an experimental admissibility interval. Rare charged environments deserve an explicit applicability caveat.

Formal-charge completeness, protonation validity and partial-charge availability are separate claims. Element-wise radius overlap may be measurable before full receptor force-field preparation. Contact candidates must retain their candidate label.

PAINS/BRENK rule names are pattern labels and can be broader than the apparent name. Report actual elements and bonds. Overlapping matches do not independently prove instability or low activity.

Global relaxation energy is not binding free energy or a local energy decomposition. Category probabilities are not calibrated defect probabilities. Stage comparisons require consistent chemistry and roles before a persistence claim.
